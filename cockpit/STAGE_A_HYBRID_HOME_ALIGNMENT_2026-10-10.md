## Decision and exact boundary
The existing `/static/cockpit/` remains the sole Home. Stage A is a UX correction to the existing V3 source, not a new app or a release. The latest integrated-design contract `UNIFIED_VISIT_V3_INTEGRATION_CONTRACT_2026-10-10.md` (frozen blob `9e107112020e2d98d542867c64fbb08d47e278dd`) remains the authority for the **two pending protected read extensions** and associated already-prepared narrow R2 request. This addendum supersedes only the *Home navigation and hybrid interaction* clauses. Do not replace the pinned read request or silently claim its review PASS.

1. **Visible first screen:** full registered-patient search on type, with backend-scoped pagination; **three** actual recent completed clinical encounters and **three** next-today scheduled appointments, no invented rows. Patient click from either central 3+3 or the search result goes to the protected selected-patient context (or appointment-only with explicit registry selection if unlinked), not an overlay. The actual protected 3+3 projections remain R2 PRE-CODE HOLD and cannot be implemented by this Stage A UI change.
2. **Visible compact left rail:** display the existing authoritative `Previous / Current / Next` projection from `/clinical/calendar/cockpit-context`, with its coverage/freshness state and calendar link. Do not create a second oversized next-patient card, a calendar writer or a new fetch. Clicking an available rail appointment opens a **floating Visit Brief** on the same page with only source-supported appointment reason/time/source/freshness while no verified clinical patient is linked. Never infer patient identity from name, booking phone or appointment ID. A user-initiated registry selection is the only existing next step; when a verified protected link becomes available, the Brief may show supported G3/longitudinal information and a genuine full-record action, under its own existing owner and affected contract. Do not mislabel appointment-only context as clinical history.
3. **Obvious permanent tool access:** visible compact direct entry to Reception, Calendar, Surgery, Osteoporosis, Physio, Sick Leave, RF, Medical Report, Learning and existing Visit Capture. Preserve currently released routes/actions, including Surgery queue sorting/editing; the long secondary section may hold explanatory/advanced content, but not be the sole entry to the working tools.
4. **Dia on demand:** keep the visible/copyable clinician prompt, browser-only three-part plaintext preview/edit/reset and no implicit protected Save. Switching patients clears transient text; no new provider access or clinical write.
5. **Clinical inbox is not faked in A:** the product decision is `Κλινικά προς έλεγχο · N`, counting *all* known unreviewed/needs-re-review clinical attention items from enabled, adequately covered sources, with deduplication and honest stale states. The initial compact Home preview is approximately 4–6, but the **full queue has no three-item cap**. Until the independent Clinical Inbox core/source adapter (Stage B+) exists, do not show an invented N, 0, sample clinical items or actionable unread-Gmail badge; label the source as not connected or simply omit the badge.
6. **Deferred B–E:** first Clinical Inbox core/manual states; then one authorized live lab-source intake with durable delivery; then clinician-approved Reception → Zadarma communications and additional MRI/X-ray/GESY adapters; then owner-backed work queue, morning/evening on-demand briefs and typed Dia/Heidi-to-protected Save. No new owner/store/provider connection or automatic task/message/booking in A.
