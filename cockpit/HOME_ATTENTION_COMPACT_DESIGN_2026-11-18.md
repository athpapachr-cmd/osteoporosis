## Compact presentation

Replace the five large source sections with a readable panel inspired by the supplied CSS/mockup. Show today's and tomorrow's pending surgery rows first, with their true date, available time, name and procedure; allow access to the remaining currently loaded surgery items and the existing full surgery list. In `Να γίνουν`, show four actionable rows initially and an explicit expand/collapse to expose the remaining loaded rows. These include dated/overdue patient obligations, RF approved without linked surgery, source-proven report-writing-started and sent-report-with-balance, and pending surgeries with earlier dates. Do not call a surgery completed or a report requested based on a missing field. The counter is explicitly for loaded rows; if sources are incomplete, say so and never imply a complete total.

Group multiple obligations ONLY by the validated existing `patient_id`, never by display name; absent/invalid patient id stays a separate single item. Different people with the same name remain different groups. Within a group, show up to two actual source `description` values, ordered by date and visually clipped in one line; each is an accessible independent control opening its own exact `pending_id` through the existing protected owner. A `+N ακόμη` action opens the existing grouped obligations view; it must not silently identify a patient by name or choose another person's record. One-patient groups remain separate from RF/report rows because these source records have no proven identity mapping to that chart.

Order actionable rows by actual relevant date, oldest first: obligation `review_date`, RF `decision_date` (only when explicitly present and valid), report `writing_started_date` or `report_sent_date` according to its displayed state, and previous surgery date. Clearly label the type of date; only patient obligations with past review date may receive a red overdue dot/late-day label. Missing or invalid dates sort after dated items and display no invented age. Date arithmetic uses Cyprus calendar dates; event age is not a medical or administrative due date. RF approval date is not a deadline.

Place count of undated/invalid surgeries and the qualified Reception `unhandled_count` in the quiet footer, linked to existing views. The calls count means not marked handled in the available bounded 200-call view, not proven missed calls or true complete backlog. Keep the accessible transient privacy toggle in the header; hiding must suppress all names and free-text descriptions (including surgery procedures) but not create fictitious initials. No patient data in local storage, public tests or logs. A combined coverage note and explicit named loading/unavailable/partial-source warning must remain visible even if other sources are populated; never show a global all-clear on incomplete data. Never discard hidden rows, unverifiable dates or source failures.
