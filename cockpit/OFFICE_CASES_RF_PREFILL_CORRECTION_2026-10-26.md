# Priority 3 RF-to-surgery prefill correction — design candidate

## Product Owner decision and observed source

The clinician asks for a separate add-to-surgery action for each RF and editable automatic birth date, patient phone and procedure type. “Date” means **date of birth**. They chose to enter birth date and phone **in RF Tracking for future RF applications**. The currently released Office Cases list already renders one button inside each unlinked `approved` or `scheduled` RF card; other stages and already-linked applications have none. Issued RF stores an exact identity number, patient name, age, site/laterality/exact location and immutable payload; it does **not** store exact birth date or patient phone. Age cannot establish a birth date. The physician-directory phone is the doctor's number and must never fill the patient field.

## Target behavior and owners

1. Extend the existing protected RF form with optional **patient birth date** and **patient phone** alongside its existing patient fields. Preserve these fields in protected saved drafts and immutable issued-application JSON, with bounded types/length and no raw values in logs or public HTML. Existing RF applications and older drafts remain readable; absent fields stay absent. No new provider, table, credential or migration. The official RF PDF layout and patient age field remain their current owners. When a DOB is supplied, the age shown on the PDF must be checked against completed years on the actual application date, with a clear correction before issuance if inconsistent; blank DOB retains current RF issuance behavior. The exact RF identity and clinician-entered source values are the only automatic demographic source in this correction. Prior RF, chart name matches and age must not synthesize DOB/phone.
2. The Office Cases RF list continues to show one **Προσθήκη στη λίστα χειρουργείων** action on each *unlinked* `approved`/`scheduled` card. The explanatory label makes that per-RF rule clear. On click, fetch that exact RF detail again under the Clinical Key and check ID, revision, stage and unlinked state before opening the surgery editor. A stale/ineligible/linked result refreshes the exact visible RF; no provisional form from the old card. Keep pagination honest.
3. The exact protected RF detail supplies only an editable `surgery_prefill` for an eligible unlinked application: saved DOB and patient phone when valid/present, plus a procedure suggestion derived from its issued exact RF anatomical location, e.g. `Ραδιοσυχνότητες — Αριστερό γόνατο`. A suggestion longer than the surgery field limit is left blank with a clear prompt, never silently truncated. The prefill is a proposal, not a diagnosis or record. Name, identity, side and any genuine RF scheduled date keep their current prefill. If an older RF lacks DOB/phone, show the missing fields explicitly and require the clinician to enter them before one existing Save confirmation. Do not derive an exact birth date from age or copy an unrelated patient's contact details.
4. Preserve the current server transaction: exact RF revision/stage recheck, one RF-to-surgery link enforced by unique FK, no automatic RF stage change, no duplicate surgery. On 409, refresh exact RF; on logout/key loss or a later RF selection, drop pending detail and prevent stale prefill or write continuation. No patient values in test fixtures, screenshots, PR prose or logs; synthetic data only.

## Finite evidence and release

R2 because RF issuance gains two private patient fields and the surgery editor proposes clinical identity/contact data. Reuse the prior Priority 3 pre-code and post-code PASS for unchanged link/payment contracts. One independent affected pre-code review of this correction, then bounded implementation. Evidence: synthetic RF draft save/open and issue/issued-copy with and without DOB/phone; age/date mismatch rejected; approved/scheduled per-card buttons and ineligible/linked exclusion; exact-click prefill and old-RF missing-field fallback; changed RF mid-click and auth/session loss; existing RF PDF and RF-to-surgery write regressions. One independent exact-code post-code R2, affected CI, guarded PR/Render deploy and no-write production smoke. No real RF/surgery write by the agent. Stop for clinician acceptance before Priority 4.
