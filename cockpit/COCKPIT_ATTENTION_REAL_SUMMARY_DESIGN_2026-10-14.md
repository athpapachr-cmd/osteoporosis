## Product Owner plain-language checkpoint — CONFIRMED

Today the "Χρειάζονται προσοχή" card is mostly shortcuts, not the requested daily work summary. Product Owner explicitly approved a compact, source-backed one-tap attention summary: on a date with **exactly one pending scheduled surgery**, show patient name and procedure; where multiple surgeries share the same day, show the day count but **no names**. Show RF issued-attempt totals, current awaiting-decision counts, recorded approval/rejection decisions, scheduled/performed states and verified own-follow-up counts, with truthful unknown/unavailable states; do not mislabel issued PDFs as submitted RF. Medical reports currently have no durable completed/pending ledger, so do **not** present fabricated counts; a separate bounded R2 report-status capability is needed. Email Inbox remains unconnected, not fake.

Concrete synthetic illustration: "Χειρουργεία · 3 εκκρεμή · 1 χωρίς ημερομηνία" followed by "Πέμ 15/10 · Ασθενής Δοκιμής Α · αρθροσκόπηση" *only* if there is one surgery that date. "Παρ 16/10 · 2 χειρουργεία" if two share that date. RF row: "Εκδοθείσες αιτήσεις 9 · Αναμένουν απόφαση 3 · Εγκρίθηκαν 2 · Απορρίφθηκαν 1 · Πραγματοποιήθηκαν 1 · Δικά μου προς παρακολούθηση 2" (illustrative numbers, never production assumptions; categories can overlap and are not additive). Medical reports: "Δεν τηρείται ακόμη ιστορικό ολοκλήρωσης". Tap surgery → existing queue, RF → existing RF workflow, medical reports → existing report workflow.

## Scope for implementation after independent R2

1. **Surgery — reuse protected loaded queue.** `static/cockpit/app.js` already fetches `GET /clinical/surgeries`, containing `full_name, surgery_date, procedure_type` and published aggregate `cockpit:surgery-counts`. Extend that same in-memory event/payload, not a new endpoint or reader. Use dates already represented as local YYYY-MM-DD values (no timezone conversion of date-only values); compare with today's Asia/Nicosia ISO date when selecting upcoming days. Preserve existing `total` and `undated`, and derive dated group sizes with a bounded upcoming preview of up to three closest date groups; include a patient's `full_name` and `procedure_type` **only when exactly one pending surgical row has that same valid date**. For two or more, count-only, never names. Show overdue-date count separately and do not infer performed/completed from date; pending queue is authoritative. No names, clinical IDs, phone, DOB, surgery IDs in URLs, local/session storage, logs, browser badges or queue state shared outside the protected Cockpit. No new linkage; tapping opens existing queue without auto-selecting a protected patient. If source is 401/403, missing, stale/failed or not an array, show unavailable/login (never zero). Count and personal detail must clear on failure and only be shown while protected source is confirmed.

   **P1-02 — request-order and authorization correction:** The existing `loadSurgeryQueue()` has no asynchronous revision guard. Before Home publishes singleton name/procedure, maintain a monotonically increasing request generation in that **same existing loader**, including initial, `clinical:authenticated` and post-write reloads. Only the latest generation may update `surgeryRows`, queue DOM, `window.CockpitSurgerySummary`, or dispatch `cockpit:surgery-counts`; optional abort is not a substitute for this guard. On an authentication refresh clear the previous Home surgery projection immediately until a fresh protected success, and on latest 401/403/503/network/non-array/invalid-data failure clear all previously displayed name/procedure, counts and cached surgery projection and show unavailable/login — never zero. Older success must not republish PHI after a newer denied request, and older failure must not erase a newer success. Validate the loaded result as a pending-row array before publishing; never synthesize a missing patient name. This is a bounded extension of `static/cockpit/app.js`, **not** a second surgery reader, identity link, persistence mechanism or new endpoint.

2. **RF — reuse existing read-intent search, no new backend or schema.** The already-protected RF `POST /clinical/clinic-utilities/rf/api/outcomes/search` accepts `{query:"",scope:"all",page:1}` and returns `counts`, `denominator`, `truncated`. Although POST, it is a read-only search and MUST NOT be confused with `/api/outcomes/{id}/events` or RF creation. Fetch on initial Home and `clinical:authenticated` refresh with same-origin credentials and no credential storage; use revision/race guard to ignore stale async responses and clear counts when unauthorized/unavailable. **P1-01 — source-accurate RF labels and coverage:** Validate `denominator` and `counts.issued_unknown/pending/approved/rejected/scheduled/performed/mine_actionable` as present, finite nonnegative integers, and require a literal boolean `truncated` (not a missing/falsy default). In the existing `list_rf_application_progress`, `counts.pending` counts **only the current `submitted` stage awaiting decision**, not all applications ever submitted; `issued_unknown` represents issued documents with unknown progression, not submitted or pending. `denominator` counts issued application **attempts** scanned, not confirmed submissions or unique patients. Display `Εκδοθείσες αιτήσεις N` and `Αναμένουν απόφαση N` only for validated `truncated === false` responses. **Never** label `counts.pending` `Υποβλήθηκαν` or derive a historical submitted total: colleagues' reported decisions can exist without a recorded submitted date. Decision outcome `approved` may overlap with later `scheduled`/`performed`, so displayed categories are **not additive**. If `truncated === true`, show `Μερική κάλυψη — άνοιξε RF`, preferably withholding all source-wide numbers rather than suggesting exhaustive totals. Missing/invalid `truncated`, malformed fields, 401/403/503/other errors or blank source must fail closed (`Μη διαθέσιμα`), **not zero**. No individual RF names or IDs enter the Home attention DOM. Link opens existing RF screen; my actionable amount refers only to explicit source-verified `mine_to_follow`, never auto-assign colleague applications.

3. **Medical report — truthfulness now, recording later.** `clinic_utilities/clinical_documents/report_api.py` currently exposes analyze/refine/research/preview/pdf with `autosave=False`, no case persistence; there is **no durable completed/pending report status ledger**. Home must say `Δεν υπάρχει ακόμη αξιόπιστη καταμέτρηση` and route to existing Medical Report, not print 0 or claim completion. A separately owned minimal protected report-status ledger (draft/complete/void and timestamp, issuer provenance, correction/retention/access rules, without raw PDF/transcript/signature storage) will require separate Product Owner acceptance and pre-code R2; **not included** in this Home-only implementation, and should not be quietly invented.

4. **Other unchanged authority.** Existing Inbox remains disconnected and badges truthful; Home search/recent 3, Visit Capture draft/Save/identity, appointment and Reception coverage remain unchanged; no new source/provider writes, no new credentials or analytics, and no extra action menu. UI remains concise at 390/680/1280px with one-tap access, no PHI in public CI artifacts: use fabricated names exclusively in test data/screenshots.
