**Base/source:** `athpapachr-cmd/osteoporosis` at freshly verified `4cf219e8f3f254caeb88134577af397afc229410`. **Owner:** Cockpit Visit Intelligence. **Priority:** now, before Cockpit Medical Reports persistence (#153). Existing Home appointment layout PR #160 is released/accepted and is **frozen**.

## 1. Product Owner's explicit reprioritization

**Problem today:** the clinician opens a real appointment and sees the patient's display name, time and reason, but the Visit Brief says there is no confirmed clinical link and cannot show what happened last visit, what was planned, or what needs checking today. Clinic reports are used less frequently; visits are used throughout each working day.

**Clinician-visible v1:** keep Home unchanged. Tap Previous / Now / Next once. The same existing floating Visit Brief shows booking reason immediately; where a **verified exact booking → registered patient link** exists, additionally show concise (a) last signed/completed visit with date, (b) latest documented plan/decisions and relevant medication actions, (c) existing follow-up/awaited items and their *last recorded* status, (d) specific previously documented checks/purpose for today's visit; plus direct entrance to the already-protected selected-patient workspace. On first unlinked appointment, an explicit one-time selection/confirmation creates only the bounded booking link; never reveal any candidate's private history before confirmation.

**Synthetic example:** “09:00 · follow-up, knee pain”. The first click shows time/reason and, after previously confirmed linkage, “Previous 22/09: assessment and explicit plan; Awaiting X recorded 22/09, status must be verified; Today: reassess Y from the previous NextContact”. If no signed evidence, show “Δεν υπάρχει προηγούμενη επιβεβαιωμένη καταγραφή”, not guessed treatment or tests.

**Not in v1:** Medical Reports #153 code/statuses, Clinical Inbox/Gmail, Reception messaging/voice/Cal booking writes, automated patient identification, invented clinical tasks/AI recommendations, GESY extraction, new patient registry, patient-data migration, changes to Visit Capture Save or the Home layout.

**Why this comes first:** repeated clinical use and existing verified protected sources. After one bounded independent affected R2 pre-code and separately authorized implementation, run finite synthetic tests, one affected exact-head post-code R2 and separate release decision.

## 2. Reuse the owners already in production (EXTEND, no competing engines)

- **Actual appointment:** `clinical_calendar.py` protected `/clinical/calendar/cockpit-context` already reads the Reception-owned actual schedule, provides opaque booking UID/time/reason, validates freshness and Cyprus-day coverage and does not confirm a protected patient. `CockpitAppointment` has no `patient_id`. Weekly `/clinical/calendar/appointments` similarly returns `linked_patient_id=None` for fresh live Reception rows; legacy `ClinicalAppointmentORM.linked_patient_id` from snapshot imports cannot be assumed authoritative for this live read path.
- **Protected clinical source:** `clinical_data.py` owns `PatientORM`, `EncounterORM`, `ClinicalPendingORM` and existing read endpoints `/clinical/patients`, `/clinical/patient/{patient_id}/encounters`, `/clinical/patient/{patient_id}/pending`. Signed Visit Capture stores an authoritative reviewed candidate in `payload_json._visit_capture_v1` with `signed=true`, decisions, next contact, medications and source/provenance. Completed/amended historical visits and draft/unreviewed sources must remain distinguishable.
- **Existing interface:** `static/cockpit/index.html` Previous/Now/Next buttons and `static/cockpit/clinical-workspace.js` floating `#visitBriefOverlay` with explicit protected registry selection. `static/cockpit/app.js` fills those same buttons. Reuse their single fetch and identity/Peek semantics; avoid auto-fetching patient summaries for all Home appointments.
- **Longitudinal context:** reuse supported G3 / signed history projections only when the clinical payload contract genuinely maps; do not copy a second independent longitudinal state engine into Cockpit.
- **Adjacent exact UID work:** RF × Secretary booking PR #159 is a separate **design-only RF-owned association** of issued RF and exact Cal booking. Appointment ↔ clinical patient link is *not* RF ↔ booking; both must normalize/trust the **same Reception booking UID authority**, with no duplicate booking provider, polling engine or name matching. #159 is not a precondition, and may not mutate or infer this patient link.

## 3. Trust boundary: one verified booking link, persistent but narrow

### 3.1 Exact source identity and controlled confirmation

- A link addresses precisely **one** observed Reception booking namespace + immutable/opaque UID, normalized server-side without guessing prefixes or deriving from name/date/phone. Server revalidates the booking against the existing authenticated, bounded, **fresh Reception schedule** under the appropriate Cyprus-local target date; missing/incomplete/ambiguous/stale provider response fails closed. Booking details remain Reception-owned.
- The clinical patient ID comes only from an **explicit user-selected existing protected PatientORM**, after checking source/person identity using independently confirmed patient identifiers/evidence, not merely appointment display name. The interface can suggest search results but never preload their history or link them automatically. If Reception currently exposes only display name, it supplies **no strong auto-match evidence**; require clinician's explicit verification against independently available source and chart identifiers. An internal `patient_id` is not automatically an official GESY/identity number.
- A single confirmation action with visible patient + booking context and explicit `Επιβεβαίωση σύνδεσης` persists booking-specific association for future opens **of that same stable UID**. Future appointments with different UIDs **remain unlinked** unless a future independently verified booking-person identity contract exists. Do not claim one manual link proves lifetime patient ↔ Reception identity.
- Store only necessary booking namespace/UID, selected protected patient ID, link state/revision, clinician confirmation/evidence **type** (not gratuitous raw national identifiers), booking identity hints/fingerprint needed to invalidate reassignments, event time and minimally attributable “confirmed via protected clinic operator” audit. Existing shared Clinical Key authenticates access but **does not establish an individual named clinician**, so never invent per-user actor identity.
- On changed/removed/cancelled booking, booking UID collision, substantive patient/identity-hint conflict, source coverage loss, link revision conflict or revoked link, **withhold patient-specific content** and offer re-confirmation. Simple rescheduling with stable trusted UID and consistent person can update displayed time from current source without silently changing clinical patient. Allow deliberate unlink/correction with optimistic version + audit; never overwrite another patient link silently. No linkage by fuzzy name, exact phone alone, date/time, recent-visit name or RF issuer.
- One narrow RF/Calendar-owned link record keyed by exact booking source+UID is acceptable **only after** comparing existing calendar import mapping and enforcing separation from mutable Reception source fields. Prefer a protected clinical/calendar sidecar in the existing configured database when the older `linked_patient_id` column cannot faithfully persist clinician-confirmed live booking links. No second patient master or external provider record writes. A table created with `create_all` cannot silently alter an existing live table. Define retention/correction/deletion and clinic access expectations within existing protected clinical policy before enabling identifiable live writes.

### 3.2 API/read contract, limited PHI

- Reuse the existing protected Calendar router and clinical data read owner. A bounded **read-intent** endpoint accepts only opaque booking UID in a protected request body, revalidates the current booking and link, returns **no clinical detail** for unlinked/invalidated/ambiguous booking. Only after the link is positively resolved may an owned, minimal patient-brief projection read that patient's clinical rows.
- A bounded **explicit** clinician-confirm link/unlink endpoint owns versioned/auditable writes *only to the association*, never to PatientORM/EncounterORM/ClinicalPendingORM. No untrusted external content can choose a patient ID or an API action.
- Do not put patient identity, details, link tokens or clinical text in browser URL/history, logging, analytics, local/session storage or a public artifact. Clinical Key/cookie rules remain unchanged. Browser request generation must prevent late response for appointment A populating B, or old confirmed association surviving logout/auth loss.
- Brief payload contains source evidence/time and signed status; finite item caps and a clear `partial/unavailable` state. Do not send entire prior encounter payload, raw Dia text or a full unbounded patient history to the Home page merely to render four concise fields. No silent cache after closing or changing appointments.

## 4. First useful Visit Brief — source-verified read, not generated advice

**Context row:** currently selected booking date/time, clinic and literal/source-cleaned visit reason; note incomplete/stale schedule independently.

**Previous visit:** latest existing **completed/amended** patient encounter with credible supported clinical content. Prefer signed `_visit_capture_v1` candidate and its date, clinical findings/change and impression *only if present*; an unspecific `visit_type` or generic completed legacy encounter is not a genuine signed summary. Clearly label unavailable vs unsigned.

**Previous decision / plan:** display only validated fields explicitly recorded in the signed candidate (e.g., `decisions`, medication action, `next_contact.purpose`). Preserve source reference, review/verification, uncertainty and actual previous date. Do not interpret a discussion as a treatment administered, or a proposed dose as an accepted plan.

**What is pending:** use the existing protected `ClinicalPendingORM` for the linked patient. Display item description, recorded status, origin encounter and review date if recorded; `resolved/cancelled` must not be presented as open. For statuses currently lacking a reliable post-visit closure/update workflow, label as “Καταγεγραμμένη εκκρεμότητα — επιβεβαίωσε αν ισχύει” rather than asserting today's truth. Do not create CareTasks or resolve them by opening a brief.

**What to check today:** show only the explicitly stored `next_contact.assess`/purpose and contextually relevant unresolved-as-recorded review items, as *proposed checks, not clinical diagnosis or advice*. If absent, show “Δεν έχει καταγραφεί συγκεκριμένο σχέδιο για σήμερα”. No LLM inference, derived deadlines or automated clinical action.

**Fallback:** if no verified link, current schedule only plus obvious patient-confirmation action; if linked but no signed history, blank/unknown honestly. If clinical source 401/403, suppress protected content and offer common Clinical Key; if data source 503, retain no stale PHI and communicate which source unavailable.

**UI constraints:** enhance existing floating Visit Brief only; one click opens; linked patient immediately shows minimal history; one-tap “Κλινικός φάκελος” enters the current protected Cockpit patient workspace without changing Save authority. At split 680px / mobile 390px retain compact slots/Home and accessible overflow. Explicit back/close never mutates patient record or any provider, and no shadow “to-do” engine.

## 5. Finite synthetic acceptance / release boundaries

1. **Identity:** unrelated same/fuzzy-name or same telephone booking cannot auto-link; verified patient selection/confirmation creates only booking-UID-scoped link, reopens same UID one-click; second UID remains unlinked; booking collision/change/reassignment, stale provider, ambiguous mapping and wrong patient fail closed. Concurrent conflicting corrections → 409, no blind overwrite.
2. **Clinical authority:** source last visit must be completed/amended and supported; signed structured decision/plan/next contact preserved by provenance; unsigned/draft and missing source states explicit. A “no history” patient receives zero inferred clinical conclusions. No entire encounter leaked to unconfirmed appointment.
3. **Pending truth:** resolved/cancelled excluded from open display; awaiting status remains “last recorded”, never proof still unresolved, no auto-close or new task. Different patients' pending/encounters never cross projections.
4. **UI and concurrency:** actual Chromium synthetic at 1280/680/390, first click Visit Brief, explicit linkage, reopened linked UID, then fast switching to another UID while an earlier protected read is delayed. No stale PHI or browser persistence, no Live calendar provider writes, no unsaved Dia/Visit Capture loss.
5. **Scope/reuse:** Home V4 PR #160 stays exactly the accepted positioning; no edits to RF #159, Medical Reports #153, Clinic Inbox, Secretary workflow, Clinical Key, or existing clinical Save; existing signed encounter/pending and Reception readers are reused. Test with synthetic patient and booking data only.
6. **Separate authority:** independent focused **pre-code R2** for the *new booking↔patient persistent linkage/read* contract; reuse the independently accepted Visit Brief + Inbox design R2 Q1–Q6 (receipt `cockpit/reviews/VISIT_BRIEF_CLINICAL_INBOX_PRECODE_R2_PASS_RECEIPT_2026-10-08.md`) and existing Unified Visit V3 identity/Peek R2; do **not** reopen the settled UI or full Inbox design.
